Foodhallen
★★★★☆ 4.4
Oud-West· Food hall· $$

Indoor food hall with 20+ vendors, all of whom display EU allergen icons. Easy to find the safe stall and stick with it. The duck wraps are reliable.

Cash + card, get there before 7pm to avoid the bar crowd.

De Pizzabakkers
★★★★☆ 4.5
Multiple· Pizza· $$

Sourdough, wood-fired, allergen-trained. The salsiccia pizza without cheese (yes, really) is great — sourdough crust holds up.

Don't book the canalside seats unless you've got two hours.

Hidden over Hype

What to skip, and what to do instead.

My signature · go here
Hyped

Anne Frank House last-minute

Sells out 60+ days ahead. Same-day tickets are a lottery you almost always lose.

instead
Go here

Verzetsmuseum (Dutch Resistance Museum)

Smaller, tells the broader story, walk-in always. The kids' section is moving even as an adult.

Hyped

Red Light District tour

Crowds, noise, a sort of awkward 'look at people's jobs' energy.

instead
Go here

Begijnhof courtyard

Hidden courtyard from the 14th century, two blocks away, almost no one finds it. Quietest place in central Amsterdam.

Survival kit

Tips for Amsterdam.

2 tips
TIP · 01

Rent a bike but learn the rules

Bikes have right of way over cars and pedestrians. Don't stop in the bike lane. Don't ride drunk.

TIP · 02

Walk the Jordaan in the rain

Empty cobblestone streets, gallery windows lit up, gabled houses reflecting in the canals. Best in shoulder season.

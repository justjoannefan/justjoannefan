Hidden over Hype

What to skip, and what to do instead.

Hyped

Massive, hot, very fragrant with shrimp paste. Hard to confirm ingredients in 38°C heat. The shopping is great; the eating is dangerous.

instead

Or Tor Kor Market across the street

Upscale produce market with prepared food in well-labeled stalls. Cleaner, calmer, English signage, fewer fish-paste dishes.

Sky Bar / Lebua

Famous from the movie. Dress code, crowded, you wait 40 minutes for a $25 drink.

instead
Go here

Just as high, better service, reservations actually honored. The bar menu is allergen-listed.

Hyped

Floating markets bus tours

Damnoen Saduak is a long bus ride for staged photo ops. Most stalls are seafood-heavy.

instead
Go here

Khlong Lat Mayom (local floating market)

Closer, real locals shop here on weekends, more variety of safe-to-eat snacks (boat noodles can be made fish-sauce-free with notice).

Survival kit

Tips for Bangkok.

3 tips
TIP · 01

PRINT YOUR ALLERGY CARD IN THAI

Not optional. Equal Eats or a friend in Bangkok — get one that says peanut (ถั่วลิสง), tree nuts (ถั่วเปลือกแข็ง), shellfish (กุ้ง / หอย), fish sauce (น้ำปลา) and shrimp paste (กะปิ).

TIP · 02

Eat at malls

Hot take: malls (EmQuartier, Siam Paragon, Central Embassy) are the safest food in Bangkok. International standards, allergen menus, AC.

TIP · 03

Carry an EpiPen on every walk

Real talk. Street food smells are airborne, the heat amplifies, and the nearest ER can be 40 minutes in traffic.

🇮🇹Florence

Italy Last visit · May 2025 Code · FLR

Renaissance city, walkable end-to-end in 25 minutes. Bistecca alla Fiorentina (the giant T-bone) is the splurge dinner — and naturally allergen-clean. The Uffizi takes a half-day; the river walk is the rest.

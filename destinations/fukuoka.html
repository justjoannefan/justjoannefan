🇯🇵Fukuoka

Japan Last visit · Mar 2025 Code · FUK

Tonkotsu ramen capital. Yatai (open-air food stalls) line the river at night. Smaller and more easy-going than Tokyo. Easy connection from Okinawa or as a flight stopover.

Famous chain, born in Fukuoka. Their ramen is pork-bone only — no seafood broth. You order via form which lets you exclude ingredients.

Solo booths. Perfect for an allergen-cautious meal — no one talks to you.

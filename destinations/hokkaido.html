🇯🇵Hokkaido

Japan · incl. Sapporo & Otaru Last visit · Feb 2026 Code · CTS

Powder-snow Japan. Sapporo for soup curry + the snow festival, Otaru for canals at dusk and music boxes in the old warehouses. Onsen towns an hour away in either direction. The soup curry scene is broadly nut- and seafood-free.

Go here

Otaru canal at dusk + Music Box Museum

The canal is best when the gas lamps light up at 5pm. The Otaru Music Box Museum (Orgel Doh) is the prettiest souvenir shop in Japan. Eat dinner back in Sapporo.

Survival kit

Tips for Hokkaido.

2 tips
TIP · 01

Sapporo Snow Festival (Yuki Matsuri)

Free, runs early Feb. Odori Park is the main strip; Susukino has the ice sculptures. Wear actual snow boots, not winter sneakers.

TIP · 02

Underground walkways everywhere

Sapporo has a massive heated underground system between the major subway stations. Memorize the map; you can spend whole winter days underground.

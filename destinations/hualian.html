🇹🇼Hualien

Taiwan Last visit · 2025 Code · HUN

Taiwan's east coast — the cliffside half of the island most people never see. Taroko Gorge is the main attraction; the mochi shops are the secret. Take the TRA Puyuma express from Taipei.

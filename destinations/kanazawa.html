🇯🇵Kanazawa

Japan Last visit · 2025 Code · KMQ

Edo-period gem on the Sea of Japan. Gold leaf everywhere (you can eat it, I do not), Kenroku-en garden, and the perfect base for a side trip to Shirakawa-go's thatched-roof UNESCO village. Slow-paced, beautiful, easier for allergies than Tokyo.

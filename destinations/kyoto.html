🇯🇵Kyoto

Japan Last visit · Dec 2024 Code · UKY

The walking-est Japanese city. Temples at sunrise (do this!), tea ceremonies, the bamboo grove. Matcha is everywhere — mostly off-limits for me — but the obanzai (kaiseki-style home cooking) restaurants are an allergy-aware dream. Slow your pace down.

Survival kit

Tips for Kyoto.

3 tips
TIP · 01

Stay 4 days minimum

Most people do Kyoto as a 1-day trip from Osaka. Mistake. Stay overnight in a ryokan with a kaiseki dinner.

TIP · 02

Walk Higashiyama at sunset

Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka pedestrian streets are emptiest 5–7pm.

TIP · 03

Bring small bills for shrines

5-yen and 50-yen coins for offering boxes. ATMs at 7-Eleven take foreign cards.

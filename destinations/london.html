United Kingdom Last visit · May 2025 Code · LGW

Dishoom
★★★★☆ 4.7
Covent Garden· Bombay café· $$

The single best allergy-aware kitchen I've eaten in. Every menu item is marked with EU allergen icons — and they triple-check at the table. The chicken ruby is dairy-free and life-changing.

Walk-ins until 5:45pm; after that the line is 90 minutes. Book the Carnaby branch instead.

Avoid the egg-and-bacon brioche — sneaky butter. Anything 'classic' is the safer pick.

Survival kit

Tips for London.

3 tips
TIP · 01

Natasha's Law

All UK pre-packaged food must list every ingredient + highlight the 14 EU allergens. The legal floor is higher than the US ceiling. Trust the labels.

TIP · 02

Get an Oyster card / tap your phone

Contactless on the Tube is automatic — cheaper than buying tickets. Daily cap is around £8.

TIP · 03

Pharmacies (Boots) are excellent

Antihistamines + EpiPens available with prescription. Worth picking up a UK pack as backup.

🇺🇸Los Angeles

USA Last visit · Apr 2026 Code · LAX

LA is the easiest city in the world for severe allergies. Mandatory allergen menus, kitchen training, and a whole vegan ecosystem that overlaps cleanly with dairy-free needs. Here’s where I eat when I’m in town.

Survival kit

Tips for Los Angeles.

2 tips
TIP · 01

Allergen menus are normal

In LA, asking for an allergen menu in a sit-down restaurant doesn’t feel like a confrontation — it’s standard.

TIP · 02

Skip Yelp, use the Find Me Gluten Free app

Even if you are not GF, the app has the most-honest allergen reviews from people who notice cross-contamination.

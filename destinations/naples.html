🇮🇹Naples

Italy Last visit · May 2025 Code · NAP

Birthplace of pizza. Loud, chaotic, the rules look like suggestions. The pizza is the most allergen-clean meal in Italy: flour, water, salt, yeast, tomato, mozz (skip), basil. Two ingredients away from being mine.

L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele
★★★★☆ 4.6
Centro Storico· Neapolitan pizza· $

Two pizzas on the menu: Margherita and Marinara. Order Marinara — no cheese, tomato + garlic + oregano. Naturally vegan, naturally allergen-clean.

Cash only. Take a number. Eat standing outside if needed.

Survival kit

Tips for New York.

2 tips
TIP · 01

Sub-allergens are tagged in chains

Sweetgreen, Cava, even Shake Shack list every allergen on the menu app. Use them when in doubt.

TIP · 02

Walk everywhere downtown

Below 14th Street it is faster than the subway, faster than a cab, and you actually see the city.

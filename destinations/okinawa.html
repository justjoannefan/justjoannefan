🇯🇵Okinawa

Japan Last visit · Jan 2026 Code · OKA

A slower, hotter, more relaxed Japan. The local food has Chinese and American influences from the postwar era — taco rice, spam, goya — which means surprisingly few seafood-heavy dishes on the standard menu.

Survival kit

Tips for Okinawa.

3 tips
TIP · 01

Rent a car. There is no other way.

Public transit on Okinawa is essentially the monorail in Naha + the Yui-Rail and that is it. A car is required for everything outside the city.

TIP · 02

Bonito flakes (katsuobushi) are in everything

They count as seafood for my purposes. Most home-cooking sauces include it — ask Katsuo-bushi nashi or write it on the card.

TIP · 03

Sun is more intense than mainland Japan

Subtropical climate. SPF 50, a hat, a long-sleeve UV shirt. Reef-safe sunscreen if snorkeling.

🇯🇵Osaka

Japan Last visit · Dec 2024 Code · KIX

Takoyaki, okonomiyaki, kushikatsu — Osaka eats louder than Tokyo. Day-trip to Nara for the deer (they bow!) and Todai-ji's giant Buddha. Easier-going than Tokyo, slightly harder for allergies than Kyoto but the kitchens are warmer and more willing to substitute.

Botejyu (Dotonbori)
★★★★☆ 4.5
Dotonbori·Okonomiyaki·$$

Old-school okonomiyaki shop, 1946. The batter is wheat + cabbage + egg — no seafood, no nuts. Default has bonito flakes; ask 'katsuobushi nuki' to omit.

Counter seats face the grill. Tourist-friendly English menu.

Survival kit

Tips for Osaka.

3 tips
TIP · 01

JR Loop Line

Like Tokyo Yamanote. Circles all of central Osaka. Cheap, English signage.

TIP · 02

Walking-friendly meals

Osaka style: small, walking meals, multiple per night. Do not commit to one big dinner.

TIP · 03

Nara: respect the deer

They will follow you. Do not tease — they will headbutt.

🌊Pacific Coast Highway

USA Last visit · 2024 Code · PCH

Not a city — a 656-mile drive down California from San Francisco to LA via Highway 1. Big Sur, Hearst Castle, Pismo, Santa Barbara. Allergy-easy: most stops have full-service restaurants with allergen menus.

Hidden over Hype

What to skip, and what to do instead.

My signature · go here
Hyped

Tour-bus Hearst Castle

Tour buses force a 90-minute rushed tour. The skip-the-line claim is misleading.

instead
Go here

Self-drive + Grand Rooms tour

Drive your own car, park at the visitor center, book the Grand Rooms tour online. Smaller group, more time, no buses.

Hyped

Driving PCH at midnight

Night fog. Elk. Cliff side without lights. Do not do it.

instead
Go here

Drive south-to-north OR daylight only

If going SF→LA: drive mornings so you do not fight setting sun. Ocean stays on your right.

Survival kit

Tips for Pacific Coast Highway.

3 tips
TIP · 01

Book Big Sur 60+ days ahead

Limited lodging, very popular. Post Ranch Inn (splurge), Big Sur River Inn (mid), Fernwood (budget).

TIP · 02

Gas up at Cambria + Carmel

Big Sur stretch has very few gas stations. Fill up before AND after.

TIP · 03

17-Mile Drive detour

An $11 toll loop in Pebble Beach with the lone cypress. Worth 90 minutes.

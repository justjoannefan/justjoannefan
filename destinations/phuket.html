Survival kit

Tips for Phuket.

2 tips
TIP · 01

Avoid the beach-cart pad thai

Default cooked in shared woks with shrimp paste. The pad thai you want is at sit-down restaurants where you can talk to the chef.

TIP · 02

Pharmacy supply is excellent

Boots and Watsons stock Benadryl, Zyrtec equivalents, and (in Phuket Town) some pharmacies will fill EpiPen prescriptions.

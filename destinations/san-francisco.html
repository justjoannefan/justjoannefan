Survival kit

Tips for San Francisco.

3 tips
TIP · 01

Bring layers always

SF microclimates are real. 65°F sunny in Mission, 55°F foggy in Sunset, all in the same hour.

TIP · 02

BART to Berkeley + Oakland

Half the food scene is across the bay. Cha-Ya for vegan, Comal for Mexican, Burma Superstar for Burmese.

TIP · 03

Avoid driving downtown

Parking is hell. Use BART, Muni, or rideshare.

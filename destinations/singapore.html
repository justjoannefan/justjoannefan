Survival kit

Tips for Singapore.

2 tips
TIP · 01

Allergen labels are mandatory

Every packaged food product carries clear allergen info in English. Even bakery items at chains like BreadTalk are tagged.

TIP · 02

Hawker centres — use the AC ones

Lau Pa Sat, Maxwell, Tiong Bahru. Cleaner kitchens, separate stalls, easier to assess each station individually.

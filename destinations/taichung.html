🇹🇼Taichung

Taiwan Last visit · 2025 Code · RMQ

Where bubble tea was invented (at Chun Shui Tang, 1986). Second-wave coffee shops on every corner, easier-pace energy than Taipei, and the Rainbow Village if you want a tourist box ticked in 20 minutes.

Chun Shui Tang (春水堂)
★★★★☆ 4.5
Multiple· Bubble tea origin· $

This is the bubble tea origin restaurant. Tapioca pearls are nut-free, seafood-free. Dairy is the variable — they have non-dairy creamer options.

Sit-down, full menu. Their stir-fried noodles are reliable too.

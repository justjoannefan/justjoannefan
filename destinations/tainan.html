🇹🇼Tainan

Taiwan Last visit · 2025 Code · TNN

The oldest city in Taiwan and its undisputed food capital. Beef soup at dawn (Beef Soup A-Kun), danzai noodles at noon, milkfish congee at midnight. The most relaxed pace of any Taiwan city — and the kindest kitchens for allergies.

Beef Soup A-Kun (阿堂鹹粥)
★★★★☆ 4.7
West Central· Beef soup at dawn· $

Open 5am to 1pm. Hot bone broth poured over thin-sliced raw beef. Zero seafood, zero nuts, zero dairy. The Tainan ritual.

Cash only. Get there before 9am or the broth-to-water ratio drops.

Du Hsiao Yueh (度小月)
★★★★☆ 4.5
Old town· Danzai noodles· $

Founded 1895 — the original danzai noodles. The base is pork + bean sprout broth, no shellfish. The classic version is the safe one.

Skip the upgraded toppings (shrimp, scallop). Stick with original.

Allergen status

Nut-freeUpgrades add seafood — order the basic

What I order

Original danzai noodle (擔仔麵), pork rib soup.

Survival kit

Tips for Tainan.

2 tips
TIP · 01

Eat your way through. That is the trip.

Tainan is famous for being a "food city" — most attractions are restaurants. Pace yourself: 5 small meals, not 3 big ones.

TIP · 02

Take the HSR from Taipei

90 minutes door to door. Cheaper than flying and you arrive rested.

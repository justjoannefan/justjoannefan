Survival kit

Tips for Taipei.

3 tips
TIP · 01

Print your allergy card in Traditional Chinese

Equal Eats does Taiwan-specific cards. The one for nuts is different from the Mainland version — make sure it says 堅果 and 花生.

TIP · 02

7-Eleven is a safe haven

Labels in Mandarin AND English allergen icons on most prepared food. Tea eggs and onigiri (no fish) saved me on jet-lag days.

TIP · 03

Skip the seafood night markets

Raohe, Shilin: airborne shrimp dust everywhere. If you have respiratory allergies, do Ningxia or Linjiang instead.

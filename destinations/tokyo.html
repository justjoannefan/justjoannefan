Survival kit

Tips for Tokyo.

3 tips
TIP · 01

Get an allergy card in Japanese AND show it

Don’t just say nut allergy in English. The cultural baseline for nuts in Japan is peanut — pine nuts, almonds, and macadamia don’t register. Use a card that lists every nut.

TIP · 02

Konbini = lifeline

FamilyMart and Lawson have full allergen labels in Japanese with icons. Tamago sando, onigiri (umeboshi/konbu), and salads are usually safe.

TIP · 03

Avoid izakaya counter seats

They share fryers and prep boards. Sit at a table where the kitchen knows you arrived and can isolate a station.

🇨🇦Toronto

CanadaLast visit · Jul 2025Code · YYZ

Canada's multi-cultural food capital. Excellent transit, walkable downtown, and EU-style allergen labeling on most chain menus. Easier mode than the US average; the Distillery District + Kensington Market are the food neighborhoods.

Cars-free pedestrian Sunday. Vintage shops, street food carts, the most Toronto thing you can do.

🇮🇹Venice

Italy Last visit · May 2025 Code · VCE

Skip the gondolas, skip St. Mark's at noon. Venice is for walking every alley until you're lost. The best food is cicchetti (small Venetian tapas) at the wine bars between 6 and 8pm. Eat with locals at the counter, not at restaurants with English menus.

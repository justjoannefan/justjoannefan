Survival kit

Tips for Seoul.

3 tips
TIP · 01

Learn the phrases for your food allergies

Memorize (or screenshot) the exact lines for what triggers you. Mine:

“I’m severely allergic to nuts.”
👉 견과류 알레르기가 심해요.
gyeon-gwa-ryu al-le-r-gi-ga sim-hae-yo.

“Does this contain nuts, peanuts, or sesame?”
👉 이거 견과류, 땅콩, 참기름 들어가나요?
i-geo gyeon-gwa-ryu, ttang-kong, cham-gi-reum deul-eo-ga-na-yo?

▶ Watch: Gwangjang allergy-safe eats
TIP · 02

Convenience stores stock allergen-labeled food

CU and GS25 carry triangle gimbap and side dishes with allergen icons in Korean — peanut, shellfish, dairy, egg are all flagged.

TIP · 03

Subway > taxi

Cheap, fast, English signage. Naver Maps is the only nav app that works properly in Korea.

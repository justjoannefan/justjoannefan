🇨🇳Shanghai

ChinaLast visit · Jan 2025Code · PVG

Mainland China's most cosmopolitan city. The Bund skyline, xiaolongbao on every corner, and the most Western-trained kitchens in mainland China — which means allergy awareness is real here. Easier than I expected for severe allergies, especially in the French Concession.

Survival kit

Tips for Shanghai.

3 tips
TIP · 01

Get a VPN before you fly

Set it up at home. Most US apps (Google, IG, WhatsApp) are blocked in China. ExpressVPN or Wireguard.

TIP · 02

WeChat Pay or Alipay for everything

Cash is dying. Foreigners can link international cards to Alipay since 2024 — set it up before you go.

TIP · 03

Metro is excellent

English signage, English announcements, cheap. Skip taxis unless late at night.
